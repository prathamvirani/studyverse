# Original activity content

Letter Lantern and Sketch Circle are original Study Space implementations with original text and interface styling. The small word/prompt arrays in `src/words.ts` were individually curated for this project from ordinary English vocabulary, without importing any dictionary, third-party corpus, protected game list or assets. They are project-authored content under the repository's licensing policy; no third-party list license or redistribution obligation is introduced. Guess validation accepts bounded ASCII English text rather than claiming dictionary membership. Custom drawing packs are bounded room input, available only to the room owner, with report/cancel moderation. No permanent drawing or competitive history is stored. Sound effects are original synthesized sine tones; no audio sample is copied.
